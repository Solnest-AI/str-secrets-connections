Your Setup Guide

You don't need to be technical — most people are running their first pricing check in under 30 minutes.

Start setup 📺 Watch the 5-min walkthrough

The promise, up front: this tool recommends, you approve — always. It reads your calendar and pricing data, shows you exactly where you're leaving money on the table, and never pushes a single price change to your pricing tool or booking system without your explicit yes. Your keys stay on your own computer. Nothing is shared.

The whole flow: install a few free tools → download the folder → open it in Claude Code → say set this up → answer a few questions. Claude does the technical parts for you, one step at a time, and checks each step worked before moving on. This guide also lists exactly where to find every API key so you can have them ready.

🔐

How your keys are handled — read this once. You will never paste a key into the chat. When a key is needed, Claude creates a small file on your computer and opens it for you; you paste the key into that file and save. Keys live only on your machine, are never committed, and are only ever sent to the tool they belong to. When you gather keys ahead of time (below), just keep them somewhere safe like a password manager.

Gather your API keys — where to find each one

You only need two to start: your PMS (booking system) and your pricing tool. Plus a free Supabase account. The rest are optional. Find your tools below and grab the keys — Claude will tell you when to paste each into the file it opens.

Pre-built

  2. Create a Personal Access Token, name it Claude MCP, full scopes → copy it (shown once)

You'll grab the Personal Access Token

Setup — step by step ~30 min

  1. 1

    Download the bundle

    Click this one-click download:

    Download the Revenue Manager (.zip)

    Or browse the project at github.com/Solnest-AI/solnest-revenue-manager → green Code button → Download ZIP. Either way: unzip it and put the folder somewhere easy to find, like your Desktop.

  2. 2

    Open the folder in Claude Code

    Open Claude Code with that folder. (Not sure how? Open Claude Code, then drag the folder into the window.)

  3. 3

    Say "set this up"

    Type set this up and send. That's the magic phrase — Claude reads the included SETUP.md and guides you from here, one question at a time, waiting for you at every step.

  4. 4

    Connect your booking system (PMS)

    Claude walks you to your PMS key (the exact steps are in the cards above), opens a file for you, and you paste the key into that file and save — never into the chat. Claude tests the connection on your real data before moving on.

  5. 5

    Connect your pricing tool (and optional extras)

    Same flow for PriceLabs (or Wheelhouse / Beyond). Optionally add Turno, RankBreeze, AirROI, or Breezeway — skip any you don't use; you can always add them later.

  6. 6

    Set up your audit database (Supabase)

    Your private log — every recommendation and approved change is recorded so your pricing history compounds. Claude creates the free database and runs the setup for you. It's your account; the data lives with you.

  7. 7

    Install the plugin and do your first run

    Claude installs the Revenue Manager, reads your existing pricing-tool min/max as your safety limits, confirms your markup, and runs your first report. Just say check my pricing — you'll get a plain-English breakdown of where you're underpriced, overpriced, and what to change, as recommendations you approve.

  8. 8

    Get your spreadsheet

    At the end of a run, Claude offers a spreadsheet. Say yes and it builds a workbook on your Desktop: a Summary tab for the whole portfolio, plus one tab per property with the full breakdown — current vs recommended prices, comps, occupancy, red flags, and reasoning. Report-only, like everything else.

A few things that are normal (not bugs)

  • You'll be asked about a second folder. If you use a tool that isn't pre-built, Claude builds a connector for it and asks where you keep your projects — point it at Documents. That's expected.
  • Restart Claude Code when asked. After connecting a tool, a full restart (quit and reopen, not reload) is sometimes needed for it to show up. Claude tells you when.
  • Your keys never touch the chat. If anything ever asks you to paste a key into the conversation, that's not how this works — keys only go into the local files Claude opens for you.
  • Some tools are plan-gated. A few APIs (PriceLabs, Smoobu, Lodgify, Turno, Breezeway) require a paid tier or an approval step — if access is blocked, contact that vendor's support.

Why you can trust it — the safety layer

Every recommendation passes through built-in guardrails before you ever see it:

  • ✓
    Floor & ceiling: never a price below your minimum or above your maximum.
  • ✓
    No wild swings: no single recommendation moves your price more than 25% at once.
  • ✓
    Honest about thin data: if the comp data is light, it says so instead of faking confidence.
  • ✓
    Currency-safe: it won't mix currencies and hand you a nonsense number.
  • ✓
    Human approval, always: it recommends, you approve, then — and only then — it logs the change. Nothing is ever pushed silently.
